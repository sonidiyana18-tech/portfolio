✎Diya’s diary — after the deadline, under the moon

A few things I wrote when a feeling wouldn't sit still. Open a page. No newsroom style guide lives here.

01 / Find me where the birds flap their wings

find me like u find flowers on a graveyard, dead and rotten but full of grief

dont rain on my parade so hard that my under eyes starts to look like the same colour as your storms. tell me to stop plucking out my pain as the petals on this rose you gave me, unbind me from the corrosion of humanity and find me where the birds flap their wings. utter your words and let the echoes sink deep beneath my skin, make me holy with your touch. grow inside me like my sadness because there wouldnt be a reason for all of this if you werent here. describe me as your favourite colour and burn the tip of the brush, paint me as your reveries. walk but dont look back, orpheus, losing me in all your lifetimes. tear my skin and stitch it with your promises. you are the teardrops ive retained in need to be bearable, every single faultline ive walked in order to keep this ordeal.

because if i say it out loud, my life wouldnt be the same anymore

02 / I wish I could dance with you on these rocks

i wish i could dance with you on these rocks and watch the city turn into dust as you proclaim your love for me

we could dance until our feet start bleeding and you could kiss the pain i keep buried in my clavicles. you say my name with a little more pain now, with your brows furrowed and withering lingering smile, it sounds more like an apology than love. love dancing like raindrops on this lake and pain swinging in the trenches, but our laughter is in the wind swirling around like a sweet symphony. you're in the wind, im in the water, nobody's son, nobody's daughter

you tell me about that song you love so much before leaving, one the scrapes out my heart for you. your fingers on my arms tells me things your mouth could never, your touch lasts longer than your love and im always alone at the end of my every poem, which is to say i worship love more than i preserve myself. if there was anything more tender than love it would be how you look at me from across the room, your eyes boring in mine so i look at the ground. the profound longing your eyes hold make me bleed and the blood makes ink for my poetries

our loves ruination, dancing at the top of the hill, making rings out of paper, holding our breathes passing through a tunnel and the imposition of my existence

you leave before the song ends and love lasts, i keep looking at the back of your head, pretending to know you will turn around, your name pulsating through my veins. i die alone this time too

03 / Like a poet loves their muse
I'll love you like Persephone loved hades in all your glory in your darkness and in your light With your demons and my light like Patroclus loved Achilles without the fates design Above destiny and universe like Icarus loved Apollo flying too close to you Flying too close to my end like Orpheus loved Eurydice being your salvation and cease my only peace like Penelope loved Odysseus in your absence with all my heart in your presence with all my soul Waiting for you for an eternity like a poet love their muse

I write the facts down carefully. Feelings, on the other hand, rarely cooperate.
